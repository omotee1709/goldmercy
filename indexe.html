About me

Storyteller first, editor second.

I studied Mass Communication at Redeemer's University, but honestly, I learned the most behind a camera. At Dove Media (then Dove Television) I spent two summers on the social team: planning the daily posts, shooting and cutting promo clips, and helping direct shoots for the music division.

They clearly liked having me around, because in November 2025 I went back to Dove Media for my NYSC service year, this time in front of the camera as well as behind it. I present on air and work as an assistant producer, so I see a piece through from the first idea to the final edit.

My internship on the social team is where I fell for short-form. You get a few seconds to earn someone's attention, so every frame, caption and cut has to pull its weight. I love that challenge.

I write my own scripts and captions, shoot my own footage and edit everything myself. I'm happiest working with a team that likes trying new ideas and checking what the numbers say afterwards.
